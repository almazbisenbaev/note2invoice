    PAYPAL_CLIENT_ID=your_sandbox_client_id

    Use the sandbox business account associated with your PayPal app. The app reads secrets only on the server.

    Your data, plainly.

    When you select Organize with AI, your work notes are sent to your configured AI provider.

    When you confirm Create PayPal draft, the reviewed client details and line items are sent to PayPal’s sandbox.

    API credentials stay on your local server. The app does not store your notes. A local request record prevents duplicate drafts; it contains a request hash, invoice ID, status, and total. Your last creation attempt and result are saved in this browser tab until you start another invoice or close the tab.
