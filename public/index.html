    Data & privacy

    Work notes are sent to the configured AI provider when you extract invoice details. The app does not store the notes on its server.

    Reviewed invoice details are sent to PayPal sandbox only when you select Create PayPal draft. No email is sent and no payment is taken.

    Credentials stay on the server. The hosted demo uses the owner’s AI key and sandbox account. Use fictional client details.

    Request records contain a hash, invoice ID, status, and total; these records are temporary on the hosted demo. Your last attempt and result stay in this browser tab until you start another invoice or close the tab.
